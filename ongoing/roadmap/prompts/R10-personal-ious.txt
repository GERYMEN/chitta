SCOPE DECISIONS (orchestrator — flag for owner):
- The IOU ledger is a PARALLEL layer. It does NOT change the original expense's
  amount in spend/insights (you paid 1000; your spend stays 1000; the ledger
  separately tracks that friends owe you 750). The ONLY insights change: a
  credit linked as an IOU repayment is EXCLUDED from income (it is money coming
  back, not earned). This keeps insights math otherwise untouched. (Alternative
  "shares reduce my spend" is more complex and double-count-prone — NOT done
  here; note it as a possible future option.)
- Directions: BOTH "lent" and "borrowed" are supported via a direction column,
  but only LENT is created from Split (you paid, others owe you). BORROWED is a
  manual entry (someone paid, you owe them). Keep borrowed minimal (manual add +
  settle); no Split-the-other-way.
