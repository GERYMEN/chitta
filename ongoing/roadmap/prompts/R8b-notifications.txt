ARCHITECTURE (follow this — it avoids headless JS and any new dependency):
- JS computes WHAT to notify and WHEN from existing in-memory data whenever it
  has fresh data (on app foreground and after a sync), then hands native a set
  of {id, title, body, whenEpochMs, channel} to schedule. Native stores them in
  SharedPreferences, sets one INEXACT AlarmManager alarm per item, and a
  BroadcastReceiver POSTS the pre-built notification at fire time (NO JS needed
  at fire time). A boot receiver reschedules from SharedPreferences.
- Reconcile each time JS computes: cancel all app-owned scheduled alarms, then
  reschedule the current set (so nothing goes stale — a paid bill's reminder is
  dropped on the next reconcile). Past-due "when" fires immediately/next window.
