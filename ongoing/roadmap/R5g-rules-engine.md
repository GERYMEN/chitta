## Pipeline (N1)

`readSmsMessage` = `combineWithRules(msg, readSmsWithReader(msg))`. The reader (`readSmsWithReader`) is the old
`readSmsMessage`, unchanged. Both run on every SMS, in memory.

| Case | Result | source |
|---|---|---|
| no pattern hit | the reader's result, unchanged | reader |
| pattern hit, reader gave no transaction and no specific class (unknown_financial / not_financial) | template | rule |
| both have a transaction and agree (type, paise, last-4 when both have one) | template record, gaps filled from the reader | rule |
| any other mix with a transaction on either side | ONE item: the reader's values (the template's when only it has one), `ruleDisagreement`, held from auto-approve | reader |
| neither side has a transaction | the reader's class | reader |
| issuer `format_verified: true` | template wins outright (set on no issuer) | rule |
| blacklist hit | not a pattern hit: a reader transaction is kept as is; a reader "unknown / not financial" becomes `otp` | rule / reader |

Rows are tagged in `parser_version` (no new column): `sms-reader-v2+rules-v<n>:<pattern_UID>` for template rows,
`sms-reader-v2+rules-v<n>:disagree:<pattern_UID>` for disagreements. Pending shows "Check this one: two readers
disagree" for the second kind. The sync result has new counts: `heldForRuleDisagreement`, `heldByMoneyKind`
(transfer / atm / wallet_load / emi / refund), `heldForChain`, `viaRule` and `viaReader`. The `sms_rescan` /
`auto_read` audit rows record counts only: viaRule, viaReader and heldForRuleDisagreement. Activity shows "N to check".

## Decisions

1. Engine body = whitespace fold only, without the reader's footer cut. This matches validate_v2 and the samples.
2. A blacklist hit is not a "pattern hit" under N1. Otherwise every real debit with a "never share your OTP" footer would be held. It only relabels a reader unknown / not-financial result as otp.
3. A disagreement where only the template has a transaction inserts the template's values, held. N1 says "reader's values", but the reader has none in that case.
4. In a disagreement, reparse extras are dropped: only ONE item is inserted.
5. Agreeing records keep the template's fields. Missing payee, last-4, ref digest and moneyHint are taken from the reader. Account kind follows the template.
6. Dates: a date-only body date on the SMS's own IST day keeps the SMS time. Another day uses 12:00 IST. A date outside [−45 d, +1 d] uses the SMS time. A missing year is inferred.
7. Confidence of a template hit: 90 needs a known or issuer-resolved sender, an amount, a pan, and a payee or set_no_pos. Otherwise 85. Unknown DLT senders are capped at 85 and short codes at 80.
8. `transaction_type_rule` values are full-match (anchored), the same as validate_v2.
9. The ref type rule is tested against the matched span.
10. Label mapping:
    - Statements: utility, insurance and pay-later are bill_due; card bills are statement.
    - Notices: mandate_future, collect_request and promo map to their own classes; everything else is bill_due.
    - Ignore reasons: failed and cheque returned → failed.
    - A transaction template on a loan, utility or insurance account becomes a non-transaction (statement).
    - create_txn false → balance_info.
11. money_kind refund is held, like the other non-spend kinds (spec). The reader's own refunds are still not held.
12. Each reparse record gets content hash `<hash>#n` and reference hash `sms-<id>-<date>#n`.
13. senders.ts got exact entries for 19 banks. NKGSB (unconfirmed) is matched by regex only. The other banks' sender_regexes stay engine-only, so broad patterns such as `^JIO...` are not taken as bank senders by the reader. CNRB-style variants are resolved by the engine, not by senders.ts.
14. Normalizer v3: "City Union Bank" still resolves to `union` through the v1 alias. CUB is told apart by its sender only. Bare "CUB" is not added as an alias.
15. Chaining suggestions are kept in session memory only, because N3 rules out a migration. Chain duplicate pairs feed R3b's pair finder: any distance, same type and amount. Refund-link and transfer-twin outcomes hold the child and are recorded, but no Review card shows them yet. "Waiting for the matching alert" is shown in Pending for 24 h. parent_override is kept on the suggestion and never written.
16. The About row is placed at the bottom of the Settings sheet. The app version reaches the picker from index.js, with a safe default off-device.
